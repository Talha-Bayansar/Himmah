# Youth privacy and safeguarding

Youth-program safeguards for the original ages 11–15 pilot; the platform also supports organizations with broader audiences. These are proposed product safeguards; the owner and mosque must confirm enrollment and local requirements before real participants join.

Invite-only membership. Use approved nicknames and illustrated avatars. Standings are visible only to authorized competition members. No real youth photos, public profiles, private messaging, location tracking, advertising or paid competitive advantages in the first release.

Private worship is not a competitive measure of religious worth. Build competition around learning, service and mosque participation; agree religious content and challenge eligibility with the mosque lead. Never generate unreviewed religious rulings or imply app rewards measure divine reward.

Peers see football attributes and team performance, not individual quiz answers, attendance histories, absence reasons or guardian information. Volunteers see only records necessary for their role. Do not shame missed weeks or remove earned ratings for absence. Offer alternative participation paths where practical; team bonuses must not create pressure to disclose private circumstances.

Before launch, document guardian consent/enrollment, minimum information needed, authorized access, retention/deletion periods, account recovery, withdrawal, safeguarding escalation contacts and incident response. Handle deletion requests without leaving unnecessary identifiable data in audit logs. Keep synthetic fixtures in source control and test environments.

No betting, entry stakes, loot-box purchases or purchasable boosts. Random training is earned in-game, with published understandable rules and guaranteed baseline progress proposed to limit frustration.

Participant invitations now record organizer identity/consent attestation and require the exact verified email. This does not verify guardian identity or establish legal eligibility. Complete the local process before inviting, use approved nicknames, and disconnect access if consent is withdrawn. The private dashboard omits staff notes, absence reasons, peer data and public rankings. Historical account links enforce no-self-approval. See [Participant access](PARTICIPANT_ACCESS.md) for the implemented boundary.
