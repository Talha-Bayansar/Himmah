# Session cash collection and balances

Open a paid session → Payments, or record payment directly beside a person in attendance. Owners/admins see each contact's original charge, cash applied, outstanding amount and unpaid/partially paid/paid status. The view is bookmarkable and independently reloadable, with URL-backed search/status/history and bounded timestamp/ID cursor pagination (25 default, 50 maximum), automatic append and manual Load more. Registration rows also show the current payment status. Attendance and cash remain independent; an unpaid balance never blocks attendance.

For the usual one-person payment, choose Record payment beside the person. A short bottom sheet shows their current balance, prefills the full outstanding amount and lets staff enter a partial payment. It shows the remaining balance and any new walk-in session fee before a single explicit Record [amount] cash save. The sheet itself is the financial review; there is no additional confirmation dialog. Successful saves close the sheet, update balances and stay on the original list with filters and scroll context. The receipt time is the save time and payer label is blank. Attendance never changes.

Choose More people or receipt details for the existing complex form on a separate page: enter the EUR cash received, optional payer label and received-on date/time in the session timezone, then explicitly allocate to one or more charges. The first selected charge prefills the amount; staff can reduce it for partial payment. Up to 50 contacts from the same session may share a receipt. Two siblings owing EUR 10 each can receive EUR 10 and EUR 5 from one EUR 15 receipt: one paid, one partially paid with EUR 5 outstanding. Family relationships never imply financial access.

Allocations cannot exceed the receipt or a charge's current outstanding balance. Excess cash remains explicitly unallocated in the session summary/history; it is not silently assigned to another contact. A receipt must allocate a positive amount to at least one existing or explicitly confirmed new session charge. Applying retained unallocated funds later, cross-session allocation, waivers, refunds and charge adjustments are future workflows; there is no online payment provider or general donation/accounting ledger.

Confirmation shows received, allocated and unallocated amounts before recording. Cash uses bounded integer cents (maximum EUR 1,000,000 per receipt); input accepts decimal dots/commas with up to two decimals. Receipt occurrence and recording times are separate. The server records the collector; no client-supplied collector or paid checkbox is trusted. All original receipt/allocation/request records are immutable.

Owner/admin receipt history includes current collector display name, payer label, received/recorded times and per-contact allocations. An incorrect entry can be reversed with a reason and explicit confirmation in a short bottom sheet. Its allocations stop counting and balances recompute, while the original record, correction actor/time/reason and immutable request remain. This is a correction of an incorrect record, not a physical cash refund; do not use it as a refund workflow. A new correct receipt can then be entered. Cancelled registrations/sessions retain charges, payments and review flags; no automatic financial reduction/refund occurs. New collection is blocked for cancelled/free sessions or cancelled registrations whose charge originated from that reservation. Historical outstanding debts in active paid sessions remain collectable after the session ends.

## Permissions and failure behavior

Owners/admins collect and correct within their organization. Manage → Staff assignments now offers a separate cash collection permission for an activity/group, independently of attendance. Existing grants default to no cash access. Scoped collectors can see/collect only their currently assigned contacts' balances; the server checks every target charge. They cannot view complete receipt history, organization-wide collected/unallocated totals, reverse entries or use family links to discover other charges. A combined payment covering a hidden contact requires an administrator. Cash-only staff can open assigned activities/sessions and use cash without attendance access.

Every request, including exact replay, rechecks membership and current collection capability/contact scope. Role changes, revoked grants, archived scope groups and ended group membership remove access. Private financial caches and open drafts clear on denial. The command locks organization, actor, activity/session and charge rows in a consistent order; amount/version validation, receipt, allocations, audit and payload-bound request receipt commit atomically. Derived charge versions count immutable allocations plus reversals, so a payment followed by correction cannot silently reuse an old balance version.

An uncertain save freezes the exact payload/key and offers retry without reconfirming or duplicating money. Known conflicts keep the draft and offer Refresh selected balances; staff must review any allocation above the refreshed outstanding amount. Unsaved leave guards, pending controls, no autofocus, normal keyboard navigation and safe same-organization return navigation follow the shared form pattern. No cash mutations are queued offline or written to public PWA caches.

## Schema and release

`0003_session_cash.sql` adds receipts, allocations, reversals and request receipts with tenant composite FKs, indexes and bounded amounts; volunteer assignments gain can_collect=false. `cash.sql` implements arithmetic/authorization/immutable-history helpers and extends the existing access command. No provider/auth schema changes or automatic financial backfill. Fresh installers include the same schema/functions. `scripts/cash-plan.mjs` emits the additive SQL transaction plan and SHA-256 receipt; verify project, branch, endpoint, database and role before applying to development/onboarding. Main is excluded. Current migration/deployment evidence is in [infrastructure history](INFRASTRUCTURE.md).

## Paid drop-ins (29 October 2026)

Payment and registration are independent settings. Paid optional sessions do not require reservations. Attendance alone creates neither debt nor cash. Attended contacts with no charge appear as No charge yet to staff with both relevant attendance and cash access. Cash-only staff use the eligible-contact picker without seeing attendance.

From attendance or Cash and balances, choose Add session charge to record the fee for later payment, or Record cash to create the fee and allocate payment in one confirmed transaction. Partial payments and sibling allocations work in either case. Attendance displays No charge yet, Unpaid, Partially paid or Paid; these statuses never change attendance. Sessions explicitly requiring registration retain that requirement.

A principal is unique per organization/session/contact. A later reservation reuses it. Cancelling an optional reservation does not invalidate an independently created walk-in charge. First registration or first charge locks session pricing. Charges and receipts retain their original IDs, amounts and history during upgrade.

`0004_participant_billing.sql` adds direct session/contact scope, optional registration provenance and immutable billing request receipts. `billing.sql` supplies read-only projections and transactional commands; `scripts/billing-plan.mjs` installs the upgrade atomically with current pricing, registration and cash functions. A failed combined payment rolls back newly created charges. Retry uses the original request and rechecks current access. No historical charges are inferred.

## Simplicity refinement — 29 October 2026

Payments has two principal totals (outstanding and applied cash), compact contact balance rows and direct recording actions. More totals reveals owed/collected/unallocated figures. A positive unallocated amount remains visible without opening the breakdown. Receipt history and corrections retain their existing scope and audit behavior. Optional payer/date fields in the full page are grouped under Receipt details; multi-contact allocation and its confirmation remain available.

Quick cash uses the same server billing command, authoritative fee and optimistic versions. It creates a missing optional-session charge and receipt atomically when explicitly shown in the sheet. It allows only positive payments up to the selected person's outstanding balance; excess cash, multiple contacts, backdating and payer details use the full form. An uncertain result freezes the original request key/payload/time for exact retry. A known conflict requires explicit balance refresh before review and a new attempt. Current permission denial closes and clears the private sheet. Draft dismissal is guarded, pending saves cannot close, and no field autofocus is introduced.

## Present-only attendance — 29 October 2026

Open a session → Attendance → Add participant → select an eligible contact. Selection immediately marks the contact present; the sheet stays open to add others and Done returns to the list. Current present contacts are excluded in SQL before pagination and hidden defensively from stale picker data. Removed contacts become available again with their current version. The list contains only present contacts, with a single present count, search, compact Remove action and payment status/actions. No status form, selection checkboxes, absent/excused/unmarked counters or roster filters. Registrations and expected rosters never imply presence.

Remove asks for confirmation and records removal without deleting audit history or altering financial records. Removing and re-adding someone preserves the existing charge/payment and does not bill twice. The public attendance write contract accepts only add/remove with person ID, version and request key; it maps to the existing versioned transaction. The storage status/tombstone and legacy historical states remain internal to preserve history and request receipts; no schema migration is needed. Old non-present records stay off the present list. Exact request retry is mandatory after an unknown outcome; the picker cannot dismiss or launch another write until that attempt resolves. Known version conflicts refresh the lists and require a new explicit selection.

Each authorized present row shows Unpaid, Partially paid or Paid and a direct Record payment action. For an uncharged optional participant, Unpaid means no payment has been recorded; the displayed figure is the session fee, not an existing debt. The payment sheet reviews the fee and received cash before its single save. Attendance never creates debt or payment. A deferred fee can be recorded through Add session charge in the row menu. Required-registration restrictions and separate attendance/cash permissions remain enforced. The separate Payments route remains available for accounting details and cash-only staff through the session more menu.
